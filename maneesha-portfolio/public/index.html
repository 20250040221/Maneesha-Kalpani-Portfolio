A little about me

Curiosity with purpose.

An international scholar exploring the intersection of technology, national security and global policy.

TECH DIPLOMACYDIGITAL FORENSICSGLOBAL POLICY

My name is Maneesha Rathnayake. I am a fully funded international scholar pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Informatics Engineering at Nusa Putra University in Sukabumi, West Java, Indonesia.

While my academic foundation is built on analytical engineering and data management, my true passion lies at the intersection of technology, national security and global policy. I am deeply interested in how data shapes modern foreign policy, digital forensics and science diplomacy.

I have an educational background from ESOFT Metro Campus and serve as an independent researcher with the Sri Lanka UFO Research Association (SLUFORA). Through specialized global policy training with UNICEF and the Asia-Europe Foundation (ASEF), I am developing my skills in evidence co-creation and cross-cultural communication.

With an investigative mindset, I am eager to connect with professionals in international relations, intelligence and diplomatic sectors.
